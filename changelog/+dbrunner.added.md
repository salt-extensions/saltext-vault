Added a `vault_db` {py:mod}`runner <saltext.vault.runners.vault_db>` equivalent of the {py:mod}`execution module <saltext.vault.modules.vault_db>`. It authenticates with the actual master token, making database secret management and credential issuance available in a master context, e.g. during orchestration or from the reactor system. Issued leases are cached in the master's own lease store.
