Added runner equivalents for most execution modules: {py:mod}`vault_approle <saltext.vault.runners.vault_approle>`, {py:mod}`vault_db <saltext.vault.runners.vault_db>`, {py:mod}`vault_pki <saltext.vault.runners.vault_pki>`, {py:mod}`vault_plugin <saltext.vault.runners.vault_plugin>`, {py:mod}`vault_policy <saltext.vault.runners.vault_policy>`, {py:mod}`vault_secret <saltext.vault.runners.vault_secret>` and {py:mod}`vault_ssh <saltext.vault.runners.vault_ssh>`. They authenticate with the actual master token, making the respective functionality available in a master context, e.g. on the CLI via `salt-run`, during orchestration or from the reactor system. The pre-existing {py:mod}`vault <saltext.vault.runners.vault>` runner now additionally provides {py:func}`query <saltext.vault.runners.vault.query>` and {py:func}`get_server_config <saltext.vault.runners.vault.get_server_config>`.
